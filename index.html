About this console

ESG Meets CPG tracks what's changing across EU/UK/US ESG regulation for consumer packaged goods, where that exposure actually sits (by ingredient, by packaging material, by retailer), and what a real alternative looks like — built on the same discipline as the Frozen Dessert Market Console: real, cited data wherever possible, and gaps labeled honestly rather than guessed at.

Most of the console is built from an internal CPG ESG regulatory briefing (Sep 2026), cross-checked where possible against primary sources (EUR-Lex, CARB, law-firm coverage of the EU Omnibus I directive). Every row carries a source and a confidence label — nothing here is invented. The Product Life Cycle Manifest is sourced differently: it's drawn from three separate illustrative decks rather than the briefing (see its own card below). See docs/data_sources.md in the repo for full provenance, what's still unverified, and known gaps.

What's in this console

Six pages, each backed by sourced data — labeled by confidence, not presented as settled fact.

Compliance Calendar

19 rows across 12 regulations in the EU, UK and US, sorted by days until each key date, with a source and confidence label on every row.

Ingredient & Material Exposure

What's regulated, and a real alternative-sourcing approach for each ingredient or material.

Packaging Data Sources

Real UK packaging and recycled-content datasets — NPWD, the Producer Register, WRAP — not yet folded into predictions.

Product Life Cycle Manifest

73 UK retail categories, laid out the way a shopper actually walks the store — click a category for its as-is life cycle, a to-be version redesigned for sustainability, and a circular-economy view of how the material could stay in use.

Retailer & Delivery Risk Assessment

14 real grocery and delivery entities, ranked least to most likely to face a regulatory or ESG-grounds legal action.

Legislative & Political Forecast

Who currently controls each relevant body, and which direction each tracked regulation is actually trending as a result.
