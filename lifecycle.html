Product Life Cycle Manifest

73 UK retail categories, sourcing through end of life, organised by the aisle a shopper actually walks. Each one traces who touches the product and in what order, then forks: what happens after normal use, versus what happens if it expires or goes unsold — the branch that drives most of a category's real packaging and food-waste exposure. Qualitative and illustrative, not supplier data; click a category to open it.
