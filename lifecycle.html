Product Life Cycle Manifest

73 UK retail categories, laid out the way a shopper actually walks the store: produce and bakery first, through to the front-of-store aisle by the checkout. Each one carries its own three diagrams, verbatim from the decks they were authored in: the as-is swim-lane map, a to-be companion redesigned for sustainability in practice, and a circular-economy loop view of how the material could stay in use. Qualitative and illustrative, not supplier data.
